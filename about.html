    More About Rootstock · Williamson, New York

    Our Roots

    Our Roots Are in Williamson, NY

    Rootstock Cider & spirits is a farmstead cidery & craft spirits distillery located in Williamson, NY. Founded in 2012 by David DeFisher, Rootstock Cider & spirits — the first post-prohibition farmstead cidery in the Rochester region — is dedicated to using only locally sourced ingredients in crafting premium ciders, perry, and spirits. With a commitment to sustainability, we utilize a solar energy system to offset our power requirements. By pairing with other local farmers, we ensure all of our natural by-products are put to good use, and returned to the land.

    After acquiring Rootstock in 2024, current partners, Brian Shadduck & Bill Hollebrandt (both proudly from Williamson), are excited to build upon the incredible Rootstock legacy the DeFisher family created, while evolving the business into its next chapter. Today’s Rootstock is proud to offer an expanded tap room with functional loft, multiple events spaces, new bathrooms, incredible food menu, a full bar, and entertainment for everyone. We’re open seven days a week…Stop by and see us!

    Our Cider

    Original Hard Cider

    Flagship Cider

    Original hard cider is a semi-sweet cider crafted from locally grown New York State dessert apples. Its yellow sapphire color gives way to intriguing apple and banana aromas. Balanced acidity and soft carbonation make this cider an excellent choice for any occasion. Great Lakes International Cider & Perry Competition award-winning cider.

    Dry Hard Cider

    Flagship Cider

    Dry hard cider is a dry cider celebration of fermentation character. The fruit forward nature of the other Rootstock ciders takes a back seat to the bread and yeasty aromas found in this classic cider. Complex like brut Champagne yet drinkable like a fine craft beer.

    Rose' Hard Cider

    Flagship Cider

    At Rootstock, we believe that when cider say’s its rosé, it should taste like a rosé! Where many other brands choose to use colorings, we blend real wine into our cider, to combine our signature cider flavors with the bright, summery profile of sparkling rosé wines.

    Plum Hard Cider

    Seasonal Cider

    One of our very first fruited ciders, Rootstock plum is seasonally available, and combines the tart, bright flavors of our estate-produced hard cider with rich, summery flavors of fresh plums. Great balance of sweet and tart on the finish will have you going back for more!

    Hopped Hard Cider

    Seasonal Cider

    Hopped hard cider is a semi-dry cider made from estate grown Crispin apples and locally grown Cascade hops. This heavily aromatic cider exhibits a bouquet of fresh lemon and pine paired perfectly with the taste of fresh picked apples. With its balanced sweetness and acidity this unique cider is sure to please beer and cider drinkers alike.

    Heritage Hard Cider

    Limited Cider

    Our heritage cider series uses apple varieties that are 40 years or older. We decided to use two of the oldest varieties on our farm in this vintage, Rhode Island Greening, and Cortland apples.

    Perry Hard Cider

    Limited Cider

    Perry is similar to hard cider, but is made entirely from pears. Made from a careful blend of old world, tannic pears and conventional dessert varieties, this modern perry is produced utilizing a number of artisan fermentation techniques.

    Ruby Mac Hard Cider

    Limited Cider

    A single varietal cider made from Ruby Macintosh apples. We wanted to challenge the perception that using dessert fruit leads to one-dimensional ciders. Not the case! With a little imagination, and our innovative approach to cidermaking, we developed a complex, rose-gold cider that presents a classic eating apple in a new light.

    EverCrisp Hard Cider

    Limited Cider

    This single-varietal cider is made entirely with late-season EverCrisp apples. It is made by arrested fermentation to lock in residual sugars and bring out fruit-forward aromatics.

    Cerise Hard Cider

    Limited Cider

    A truly unique product at our cidery. We grow more than just apples on our farm– in fact, tart cherries are one of our biggest crops. This is a sparkling wine made entirely from fresh cherries harvested in early July. This limited-release dessert wine is best served chilled.

    Fruit-based spirits

    Our Distillery

    As a farm distillery, Rootstock produces premium quality, fruit-based spirits, using only hand-picked fresh fruit from DeFisher Fruit Farms and other locally sourced all-natural ingredients. Rootstock has a strong belief in being environmentally friendly – we utilize our byproducts on the farm, so there is no waste products to dispose of and we employ solar energy to power our production facility and tap room.

    At Rootstock, we’re dedicated to providing only the finest spirits fashioned by tried and true methods. We make it a personal commitment to craft a product that is a shelf above the typical production method.

    Enjoy our spirits with your mixer of choice, in your favorite cocktail, on the rocks, or even neat!

    Applejack 2-year, 5-year, 10-year Anniversary

    Applejack, one of the great American spirits, has been around since alcohol was produced in the colonial era, before our country was even founded. Our applejack is produced using 100% fresh apples from DeFisher Fruit Farms, and is aged in former bourbon barrels from a famed Kentucky bourbon distillery.

    It starts with an aroma of nutmeg and butterscotch that changes as it sits in the glass to more of a molasses note. A bit of subtle spice to begin, combined with a savory toasty and oaky body, our applejack finishes smooth and warm with subtle undertones of apple. 

    Available in 750mL bottle.

    Vodka - Original, Peach, Blueberry, and Raspberry

    Our Rootstock vodka is a super premium, small batch vodka distilled from fresh, locally-grown apples. Being one of the very few vodkas in the world crafted using 100% all-natural, gluten-free, locally-grown NY apples, Rootstock vodka has a distinct, fresh & delicious, slightly sweet taste, with a purity that is unmatched by the mass-produced grain based vodkas.

    Our vodka goes through an extensive 24 plate distillation process, creating a product that has an incredibly balanced and unique character; one that can only be achieved by using such a premium raw product - fresh apples.

    Available in 750mL bottle.

    Brandy Apple, Plum, Pear, Cherry

    Apple brandy is done French Calvados style, representing a blend of over a dozen apple varieties and aged in toasted red wine barrels from a local Finger Lakes winery, to offer a completely different mouth feel and finish than most American spirits.

    Plum brandy, our nod to the little known Slavic spirit, slivovtiz, is made with 100% heritage blue plums and fermented using the whole fruit. The final product never touches oak - instead it is rested in stainless steel or glass for 3-6 months and bottled at 80 proof. It smells strongly of plum and tastes like blue if colors had taste (or so our distiller says).

    Pear brandy rests in oldest former bourbon and applejack barrels for a little over 6 months, allowing the spirit to mature with wood extract. This allowed the spirit to pick up just a hint of honey on the taste, and a pale coloration that is reminiscent of the pears it was made from.

    Cherry brandy is made with estate grown Montmorency tart cherries, known to improve sleep quality, as one of the few foods that contains natural melatonin and one of the very, very few that has enough to provide benefit. So grab a nightcap of cherry brandy, and enjoy a great night’s rest. 

    Available in 375mL bottle.

    Liqueur - Coffee & Maple Orange

    Our Rootstock coffee liqueur is the perfect addition to your “cup of joe” any time of day. Made with coffee, vanilla, chocolate, and chili peppers, its sure elevate your coffee and warm the body.

    Our Rootstock maple orange liqueur is made with our apply brandy, maple syrup, and orange peel, providing an incredibly unique combination that goes great in a cocktail or on its own. 

    Available in 375mL bottle.
