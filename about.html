More About Rootstock · Williamson, New York

Our Roots

David DeFisher founded Rootstock in 2012 as the Rochester region’s first farmstead cidery since Prohibition. In Williamson, we make cider, perry, and fruit spirits with locally sourced ingredients. Solar power helps offset our energy use, and we work with local farmers to put production byproducts back to use.

Williamson natives Brian Shadduck and Bill Hollebrandt acquired Rootstock in 2024. They have built on the DeFisher family’s work with an expanded taproom and loft, private event spaces, a full bar, a food menu, and live entertainment.

From New York orchards

Our Cider

Explore the flagship, seasonal, and limited releases featured in our cider lineup. Availability can vary by season.

Flagship

Original is an award-winning semi-sweet cider from New York dessert apples, with soft carbonation and balanced acidity. Dry leans into bready, yeasty fermentation notes. Rosé blends cider with real wine for a bright, sparkling rosé character.

Seasonal

Plum pairs estate cider with fresh plum and a sweet-tart finish. Hopped is semi-dry and combines estate Crispin apples with locally grown Cascade hops for lemon and pine aromas.

Limited Releases

Heritage showcases older apple varieties, including Rhode Island Greening and Cortland. Perry blends tannic and dessert pears. Ruby Mac is a single-variety Ruby Macintosh cider, and EverCrisp retains some of the late-season apple’s natural sweetness. Cerise is a sparkling dessert wine from fresh tart cherries, best served chilled.

Fruit-based spirits

Our Distillery

We distill hand-picked fruit from DeFisher Fruit Farms and use other locally sourced ingredients. Solar energy helps power production and the taproom, and production byproducts go back into use on the farm. Enjoy our spirits neat, over ice, or in a cocktail.

Applejack

Made from fresh DeFisher apples and aged in former Kentucky bourbon barrels. Our range includes 2-year, 5-year, and 10-year anniversary expressions. Look for nutmeg, butterscotch and molasses on the nose, toasted oak and spice on the palate, and a warm apple finish.

750 mL bottles.

Vodka

Small-batch vodka made with locally grown New York apples and a 24-plate distillation process. It has a clean, gently sweet character and comes in Original, Peach, Blueberry, and Raspberry.

750 mL bottles.

Brandy

Apple blends more than a dozen varieties in a Calvados-inspired style and ages in toasted Finger Lakes red-wine barrels. Plum is fermented from whole heritage blue plums and rested without oak. Pear rests for just over six months in former bourbon and applejack barrels, picking up a hint of honey. Cherry starts with estate-grown Montmorency tart cherries.

375 mL bottles.

Liqueur

Coffee combines coffee, vanilla, chocolate, and chili peppers. Maple Orange combines Rootstock apple brandy with maple syrup and orange peel.

375 mL bottles.

The people behind Rootstock

Our People

Brian Shadduck

Partner & General Manager

Brian grew up in Williamson and joined Rootstock in 2016. After working in sales, distribution, and the tasting room, he now leads the team, daily operations, and business growth.

Bill Hollebrandt

Partner & Facilities Manager

Bill grew up in Williamson and brought more than 15 years of construction experience to Rootstock’s renovation. He manages the property and leads cornhole and line dancing with his wife Jen.

Kristen Dennie

Partner · Events, Merchandise & Social Media

Donnie Fornataro & Rocco Petrus
